The X-Plane Scenery Gateway is a mostly community-contributed service that allows the community to create and update the default scenery for airports within X-Plane, which are officially released with each update. Laminar heavily relies on the Gateway as they are a very small team and would not be able to keep every single airport in the world up-to-date on their own.

As a flight simmer who enjoys doing this kind of stuff, I contribute my own updates to the Gateway every now and then. After all, a simulator should be as accurate as possible.
