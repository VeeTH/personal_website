Douma's Backstory

From birth, Douma had rare rainbow-colored eyes and pale hair. Features like this were not normal. This led his parents to believe he was a divine being who could speak with the gods.

His parents therefore made him the leader of the Eternal Paradise Faith, a cult that worshipped Douma as a divine leader. This forced him to listen to his followers' problems and grievances and give them advice, despite being just a child.

Douma was subjected to profound emotional neglect, never forming true emotional bonds with anyone. Even including his parents, who only saw him as a tool for their cult.

While still a child, his mother, driven insane by his father's numerous affairs with female cult members, murdered her husband before poisoning herself. He felt no emotional impact.

Years later, as an adult, Douma met Muzan, who recognized his strength and lack of empathy. Douma accepted his offer to become a demon. He continued to lead his cult with a new purpose...

He would valiantly save his followers from their pain and suffering by devouring them, for they would live forever inside him.

Blood Demon Art Techniques

Douma has a wide range of techniques he can perform with Cryokinesis.

  • Barren Hanging Garden: A series of 8 to 10 successive fan attacks that create torrents of razor-sharp ice shards.

  • Freezing Clouds: A large, wide mass of clouds chilly enough to freeze the lungs of anyone who inhales them.

  • Lotus Ice: Huge, frozen lotuses with razor-sharp ice petals.

  • Scattering Lotuses: A blizzard of long-ranged ice shards shaped like lotus petals capable of tearing a person's flesh to shreds.

  • Lotus Vines: Large frost vines that extend from ice lotuses, which can capture and freeze caught opponents.

  • Wintry Icicles: A series of ice spears/stakes meant to impale his target from any direction.

  • Cold White Princesses: Two female humanoid figures able to blow freezing wind towards their intended target. They have a larger and wider range than Freezing Clouds. They can record and save whatever they see.

  • Crystal Miko: Miniature ice replicas of himself, possessing the same combat prowess and Blood Demon Art techniques as Douma himself. They can record information and relay it back to Douma.

  • Hoar – Frost Lily Bodhisattva: A large Bodhisattva ice statue, which can deal huge amounts of physical damage due to its sheer size and weight, and is also extremely durable. It can generate huge gusts of deadly ice powder and launch a barrage of ice attacks.
